## Findings

### 1. A number with the wrong denominator ⛔ fixed

**Published:** *"a full-corpus sweep is 7720 DNS lookups at worst against a
ceiling of 100,000"*, in [T-026](../../TODO/measurement.md),
[`gates.md`](../gates.md) and `PROGRESS.md`, and computed by
`politeness.run_cost` itself.

**Re-derived:** 965 distinct hosts x 8 queries. ⛔ **206 of those 965 are
address literals and cost no DNS at all.** `bep34.Resolver.consult` returns
`ALLOW` for a literal without asking anybody -- there is a test named for it --
and `getaddrinfo` resolves one without a query. The count belongs over the
**759 name-addressed** hosts.

**Corrected to 6072**, which is 6.1% of the ceiling rather than 7.7%.
`run_cost` now reports `resolvable_hosts` beside `hosts`, two tests assert that
a run of literals costs zero and that a mixed run charges only the names, and
the mutation that charges literals again fails both.

⚠ The direction matters less than the shape: this is the same defect as the
committed sweep record whose `counts.corpus` said 200 against a corpus of 1327,
which the previous session found. A denominator is the easiest number to state
without checking.

### 2. A rate quoted without the sample that makes it meaningless ⛔ fixed

The identity experiment reported a spread of **0.044** across four arms and
`--expect-arms` was ready to call anything over 0.2 material. Re-read against
the run: the arms had **50, 47, 45 and 55 contacted subjects and 1, 3, 1 and 3
answers**. Two failures in an arm of seven move a raw rate by 0.29.

So the instrument would have declared a material difference on noise. It now
requires **20 contacted subjects per arm** before it compares them at all and
prints `NO VERDICT` otherwise, and the second run -- 32 live subjects, 30
answers, descriptive **15 of 15** -- is reported with that verdict withheld.

### 3. A claim about a run's provenance that the run could not support ⚠ recorded

The first identity run was still in flight while `src/trackers/probe.py`
changed under it. Its conditions block says `0fecf12...-dirty`, which is
accurate, but the tree it names is not the tree that ran.

Not fixable after the fact and not worth 200 more requests to re-take, so the
entry states it: the change that landed refuses an answer whose socket reached
this machine, and **no row in that result did**. Checked rather than assumed.

### 5. Two record edits that were never written ⛔ fixed

`PROGRESS.md` still said one identity rotation had run when two had, and still
sent the next session at "roughly twelve thousand requests". Both edits had
been made in a script whose later assertion aborted the write, and the printed
"ok" belonged to an earlier line.

⛔ **A summary is a claim like any other**, and this is the lens's own example:
the claim "I updated the record" was false until `git diff` said otherwise.
Fixed in `c4eddb5`, and every subsequent record edit was made with a tool that
reports what it did.
