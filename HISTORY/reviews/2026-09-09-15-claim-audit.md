## ⛔ Finding 1 -- a claim of mine was read with the wrong key, by me

Auditing the published labels I wrote `t.get('health')` and got `None` for all
1326 rows, which would have read as "the dataset publishes no health at all".
The field is `health_state`. ⭐ **The audit caught the auditor**, which is the
argument for lens 3 being a re-derivation rather than a re-reading: had I
trusted the first query I would have filed a critical defect that does not
exist.

## ⛔ Finding 3 -- the test count drifted four times in one session

`check-citations.py` caught it every time -- 513 to 530 to 537 to 547 to 566 to 578 --
which is the mechanical half doing its job. ⚠ Worth recording that the check
only fires on a line **without** a `test_` token on it, so a count split across
two lines from its command escapes it. Three of this session's write-ups hit
that and were reflowed onto one line rather than exempted.

## ⚠ Finding 4 -- two figures in the record are older than the tree

`experiments/19`'s committed run reports `desirefire_all` at **1091** entries;
today's fixtures parse **1088**, and the live upstream **1074**. Nothing quotes
1091 as current -- the registry's band contains all three and
`HISTORY/corpus-baseline.md` names the run behind each figure -- but a reader
comparing the entry text to a fresh run will see the difference. It is fixture
drift, not a defect, and it is now the reason
[T-103](../../TODO/sources.md)'s history exists: the distribution replaces the
single number.
