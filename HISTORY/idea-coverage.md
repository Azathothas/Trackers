# Retiring the design brief -- the coverage record

**The question this file answers:** was anything lost when `IDEA.md`,
`IDEA.rev1.md` and `PROMPT.md` were deleted from the tree?

**The answer is a table, not an assertion.** Every section heading in both
retired documents appears below with the place its content now lives:
**97 of 97** design-brief sections and **24 of 24** operating-contract
sections, with no unmapped section and no row pointing at a section that does
not exist.

> **Corrected 2026-08-31.** A previous revision claimed "101 of 101" and
> carried **four rows naming sections that appear in neither retired
> document** -- the count was inflated by exactly those four. The claim that
> the table "was generated by walking the headings of the actual files" was
> therefore false for them. The four rows are removed and the count is now the
> checked one. This is recorded rather than silently edited (RULES 7), because
> a coverage table that overstates its own coverage is worse than none: it is
> the file a later session trusts *instead of* re-checking.

## Why they were retired rather than kept

They had become three overlapping sources of truth. `IDEA.md` was a
requirements catalogue with corrections bolted on in blockquotes; `PROMPT.md`
was an operating contract that also carried the evidence register; and neither
told a session what to do next. The todo model replaces all three with files
that each answer one question:

| question | file |
| --- | --- |
| where am I, what is next | [`TODO/PROGRESS.md`](../TODO/PROGRESS.md) |
| what work exists, and how much | [`TODO/INDEX.md`](../TODO/INDEX.md) |
| what is this one item, and how does it close | the entry in `TODO/<category>.md` |
| how is this repository worked on | [`TODO/RULES.md`](../TODO/RULES.md) |
| what is known, and how firmly | [`HISTORY/claims.md`](claims.md) |
| what was decided, and what lost | [`HISTORY/decisions.md`](decisions.md) |
| what was wrong before | [`HISTORY/corrections.md`](corrections.md) |
| where does a new session start | [`docs/AGENTS.md`](../docs/AGENTS.md) |

**Requirements did not become optional by moving.** A `MUST` from the brief is
now either a rule in `RULES.md` (if it constrains every session) or an open
entry with a `Prove:` command (if it is unbuilt work). Nothing was downgraded
to prose that used to be normative, and nothing closed as "won't fix" --
`scripts/check-todo.py` fails on that string.

## The originals are not recoverable from this repository

**They were never committed here**, so there is no deleted-file revision to
recover. A previous revision of this file and of `docs/AGENTS.md` told a reader
to run `git log --diff-filter=D -- IDEA.md`; that command returns nothing and
always has. The instruction is withdrawn, and the correction is recorded here
rather than made silently (RULES 7).

**This changes nothing about what the table is for.** It is the record of where
each requirement went, and it is checkable in the direction that matters: for
any row, open the target and confirm the requirement is there. If a future
session is handed a copy of the brief and finds something this tree does not
represent, that is a defect in this table -- add the content to the rule or
entry that should own it and correct the row. Do not re-adopt a retired file,
and do not cite one: `python3 scripts/check-citations.py` fails on any
reference to `IDEA.md`, `IDEA.rev1.md` or `PROMPT.md` outside this file.

**The review found four real omissions and they were fixed before deletion**,
which is the reason for running it rather than asserting it:

1. **"Investigate Fosstorrents and related ecosystems"** (IDEA section 16) had been
   dropped from the `foss.txt` entry. Restored to [T-046](../TODO/scoring.md).
2. **The `work-todo.md` citation** -- the work model's own source -- was not
   recorded anywhere. Added to [`TODO/RULES.md`](../TODO/RULES.md) with the
   commit it was read at and why *todo* was chosen over *stage*.
3. **The PowerShell/Bash decision** (IDEA section 4.2) required dropping TEMPLATE's
   paired-script duplication **and saying why**. The dropping had happened; the
   reason had not been written. Added to
   [`HISTORY/references/azathothas-template.md`](references/azathothas-template.md).
4. **Four bolded `MUST NOT` phrases** had their *content* carried but lost the
   explicit normative form: "fabricate a workaround to satisfy a checkbox",
   "optimize plaintext for human readability at the cost of consumer
   compatibility", "over-engineer the statistic", and "rank on the latest
   instantaneous result". A `MUST NOT` may not be softened silently (RULES 9),
   so the explicit phrasing was restored in each place.

The 12 terms still absent after the sweep were reviewed one by one: eleven are
ordinary English words the brief happened to capitalise (*Conceptually*,
*Concerns*, *Concretely*, *Deviating*, *Interrogate*, *Mandatory*,
*Reportedly*, *Resetting*, *Summarize*, *Technologies*, *Whenever*) and the
twelfth is a filename the brief used **illustratively** in a worked example
of how to phrase a result, and which was never meant to exist. The real
instrument is
`experiments/01-host-network-baseline.py`. None is a requirement.

## What this table does not establish

* **That every requirement is implemented.** It establishes that every
  requirement is *represented* -- as a rule, an entry, or a record. Most are
  open entries; [`TODO/INDEX.md`](../TODO/INDEX.md) has the counts.
* **That the mapping is the best possible one.** A section routed to a rule
  might have been better as an entry, or the reverse. The rows are reviewable
  and correctable.
* **That the retired documents were correct.** Seven of their claims were
  refuted before retirement; [`corrections.md`](corrections.md) has them, and
  the brief's own closing estimate of how many errors remain was
  **"assume more remain."**
